How we check every deal

  1. Find it live. Each price was read on 27 November 2026 from the store or from a deals source that names the store.
  2. Check the “was” price. The discount is worked out from the two prices shown — if they don’t add up, the deal doesn’t go on the board.
  3. Call it honestly. NEW LOW means the source reports a record or season low. STRONG DEAL is a deep cut below the usual price. ROUTINE SALE is a modest cut, or a price the product hits often.
  4. Show the receipt. Every card says when its price was checked and where it was read, so you can verify it yourself.
